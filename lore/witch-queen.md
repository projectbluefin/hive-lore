# Destiny lore reference — The Witch Queen era

Canon source notes for Season of the Blueberries title-slide subtitles.
Every entry has a stable ID (the `##` heading slug) that
`vocab/season-one.yaml` references through `lore_refs`. `tests/test_titles.py`
fails if a `lore_refs` value has no entry here.

Canon means: stated by the game, its Grimoire/lore books, or Bungie's own
publications. `canon_inspired` and `extrapolation` candidates in the vocab are
built *from* these notes but make no canon claim of their own.

## enclave

**The Enclave** is a location on Mars introduced in *The Witch Queen*. It
holds **the Relic**, the artifact where the player performs weapon **Shaping**
and **Reshaping** (the crafting system added in the expansion).

Source: Destinypedia, "Enclave" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Enclave>;
Bungie, The Witch Queen expansion page — <https://www.bungie.net/7/en/Destiny/WitchQueen>

## relic-shaping

**Shaping** at the Relic lets a Guardian build a weapon from a pattern;
**Reshaping** revisits a shaped weapon to change it. Crafted weapons level up
through use and can be re-forged. The Relic is the only place this happens.

Source: Destinypedia, "Weapon crafting" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Weapon_crafting>

## mars-returned

Mars was removed from the destination map at the start of *Beyond Light*
(November 2020) when the Darkness's Pyramids arrived, and **returned in *The
Witch Queen* (February 2022)** changed — partly displaced in time, occupied,
and scarred.

Source: Destinypedia, "Mars" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Mars>

## savathun

**Savathûn, the Witch Queen** — born Sathona of the Osmium Court — is the
Hive god of cunning and deception. In *The Witch Queen* she is revealed to
have taken the Light for herself and her **Lucent Brood**.

Source: Destinypedia, "Savathûn" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Savath%C3%BBn>

## lucent-brood

The **Lucent Brood** are Hive who bear the Light: they are raised by Ghosts,
use Light abilities, and can be resurrected by their Ghosts — the exact
mechanics that define Guardians.

Source: Destinypedia, "Lucent Brood" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Lucent_Brood>

## chosen

The central moral question of *The Witch Queen*'s campaign is whether the
Hive were **chosen** by the Light or took it. Ghosts choose their partners;
Savathûn argues her brood deserved to be chosen.

Source: Destinypedia, "The Witch Queen" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/The_Witch_Queen>

## books-of-sorrow

**The Books of Sorrow** (Grimoire, *The Taken King*, 2015) are the Hive
scripture: the record of Aurash, Sathona, and Xi Ro of the Osmium Court,
their bargain with the Worm gods, and their transformation into Oryx,
Savathûn, and Xivu Arath. The Books are presented as an **account kept so
that it is remembered** — the Hive treat the written record as a sacred duty.

Source: Ishtar Collective, Books of Sorrow —
<https://www.ishtar-collective.net/categories/books-of-sorrow>

## sword-logic

**The Sword Logic** is the Hive's proving principle: existence is earned by
defeating what challenges you, and **what defeats you proves it deserved to**.
Defeat is information about fitness, not merely an ending. (Canon's framing
is literal and brutal; this project uses it only as a *resilience* metaphor.)

Source: Ishtar Collective, Books of Sorrow (passim) —
<https://www.ishtar-collective.net/categories/books-of-sorrow>;
Destinypedia, "Sword Logic" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Sword_logic>

## worm-gods

The **Worm gods** — Yul, Eir, Ur, Xol, and Akka — struck the bargain that
created the Hive: power and eternal life in exchange for a **tithe** of
violence; a Hive who stops feeding their worm is consumed by it. Oryx slew
**Akka, the Worm of Secrets**, to take the power to Take.

Source: Ishtar Collective, Books of Sorrow —
<https://www.ishtar-collective.net/categories/books-of-sorrow>;
Destinypedia, "Worm" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Worm>

## osmium-court

The **Osmium Court** was the kingdom of Aurash's people on the gas giant
Fundament before the Hive existed — the original court from which the three
sisters were driven. Hive hierarchy after the bargain kept a courtly shape:
a ruling family, tribute owed upward through the chain.

Source: Ishtar Collective, Books of Sorrow —
<https://www.ishtar-collective.net/categories/books-of-sorrow>;
Destinypedia, "Fundament" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Fundament>

## the-witness

**The Witness** was revealed in *The Witch Queen*'s final cutscene: the voice
behind the Darkness, master of the Black Fleet, and the being whose attention
Savathûn spent her existence hiding from.

Source: Destinypedia, "The Witness" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/The_Witness>

## mara-sov

**Mara Sov**, Queen of the Awoken, allied with the Vanguard through *Season of
the Lost* (2021) to perform the exorcism separating Savathûn from her worm —
an alliance of necessity between powers that do not trust each other.

Source: Destinypedia, "Mara Sov" — <https://www.destinypedia.com/Mara_Sov>

## vow-of-the-disciple

**Vow of the Disciple** (March 5, 2022) is *The Witch Queen*'s raid: a
six-player fireteam enters the sunken Pyramid in Savathûn's throne world to
face **Rhulk, First Disciple of the Witness**.

Source: Destinypedia, "Vow of the Disciple" —
<https://www.destinypedia.com/Vow_of_the_Disciple>

## blueberry

**Blueberry** is long-standing Destiny community slang for a matchmade
stranger — a player on your team whose name you do not know. It is neutral to
affectionate, and it is the seed of the season title: the season celebrates
the contributors who were strangers to the project once. This is community
usage, not in-game canon.

Source: community usage; e.g. Destiny 2 subreddit glossary discussions of
"blueberry" — <https://www.reddit.com/r/DestinyTheGame/> (term of art)
